1. ZERO DATA COLLECTION

Aegis Voice is engineered with a Zero-Knowledge Architecture. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, voice recordings, or text transcripts.

2. PHYSICAL ISOLATION

The App has explicitly removed the Android Network Permission. It is technically impossible for The App to transmit data to any cloud server.

3. LOCAL PROCESSING & MICROPHONE PERMISSION

All voice recognition is performed locally on your device using our proprietary embedded Dual-AI architecture. Your voice data never leaves your phone's RAM.

To function, the App requires access to your device's Microphone. This access is strictly used to capture audio for real-time local transcription. The audio stream is processed instantly in RAM and is never saved to storage or transmitted.

4. PAYMENT PROCESSING

We do not process payments or store credit card details. All transactions are handled securely by Google Play's billing system. We only receive an anonymous purchase token to verify your license status. We do not know who you are.

5. ANALYTICS & LOGS

We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase). The App does not generate any usage logs that are transmitted off-device.

6. DATA DELETION

Since we do not collect, store, or transmit any user data to our servers, there is no user data to request deletion for. If you wish to clear your local usage data (such as settings), simply uninstall the App or clear the App storage in your Android settings.
