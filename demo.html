/// PERFORMANCE NOTE: WHY CAN'T I SEE THE "QUEUE"?

The "QUEUE" indicator is hidden by default. It only triggers when the system buffer exceeds 5+ audio slices.

If you never see this warning during your recording session, DO NOT PANIC.
It simply means your device's NPU is powerful enough to handle real-time processing with zero backlog.
Absence of this warning is the ultimate proof of your hardware's performance.
